0x00 // ABOUT

About

I graduated in Electrical & Electronics Engineering and spend most of my time writing bare-metal Embedded C — the kind of code that has to run on time, every time. My focus is the boundary between hardware and firmware: setting up peripherals directly through registers instead of vendor HALs, writing interrupt-driven control loops, and debugging with an oscilloscope in hand rather than guessing from a log file. Most of my project work has centered on motor drives and power electronics on the TI C2000 DSP, where timing mistakes show up immediately as a burnt MOSFET rather than a stack trace.
